### 🎙️ Featured University Research & Projects

#### 🧠 [PsyVoiD: Multi-Modal Psychological Coping Analysis](https://github.com)
*Sequential Mixed-Methods Pipeline Integrating Vocal Acoustics & Natural Language Processing*

- **Overview:** Developed an end-to-end multi-modal machine learning pipeline that predicts psychological coping mechanisms by combining speech acoustic features with transcript text embeddings and personality scales.
- **Methods & Tech:**
  - **Acoustic Feature Extraction:** Formant frequencies, jitter, shimmer, pitch trajectory, and MFCCs using `librosa` and `praat-parselmouth`.
  - **Text NLP Embeddings:** Contextual semantic analysis using Transformer models (`Hugging Face`, `PyTorch`).
  - **Statistical Modeling:** Multivariable classification pipelines, feature selection, and ensemble modeling in **Python** & **R**.
- **Impact:** Demonstrates the potential of non-invasive voice biomarkers for mental health assessment and clinical decision support.
